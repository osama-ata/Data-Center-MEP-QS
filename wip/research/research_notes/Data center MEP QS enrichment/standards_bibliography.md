# Standards Bibliography: current editions and BibTeX for Data Center MEP-QS

Research date: 23 Nov 2026. Status legend used below:
VERIFIED = edition/date confirmed by a publisher or national-body
catalogue page in search results this session; PARTLY = edition seen in
retailer/secondary listing only; UNVERIFIED = from background knowledge,
not confirmed this session (must be checked before citing). Catalogue
URLs marked "(URL unverified)" were not seen in results and follow the
publisher's usual pattern.

## Current edition, title, publisher and catalogue URL for each standard

### Takeaway
Most cited standards have a confirmed current edition; several have
moved since the book's outline baseline: IEC 62305 series (Ed. 3, 2024),
IEC 60364-1 (Ed. 6, 2025), UL 9540 (3rd ed., 2023), UL 9540A (5th ed.
2025, and a 6th ed. reported March 2026), TIA-942-C (May 2024),
TIA-607-E (May 2024, supersedes 607-D), NFPA 70 (2026), NFPA 2001
(2025), FIDIC Silver Book (2017 reprinted 2022 with amendments), and
BS 1192-4 (withdrawn; replaced by BS EN ISO 19650-4:2022).

### Cited Findings

#### Fire protection (Volume 4)

- **NFPA 2001, Standard on Clean Agent Fire Extinguishing Systems,
  2025 edition** (NFPA). VERIFIED (retail) as most recent; TIA 25-1 to
  4.3.4/A.4.3.4 (egress time study) issued 15 Apr 2026, effective
  5 May 2026. 2022 ed. superseded. Scope note: total-flooding and
  local-application clean-agent system design, quantity, enclosure
  integrity and testing; drives agent mass, cylinder count and room
  integrity test line items for white space. —
  [ANSI Webstore NFPA 2001-2025](https://webstore.ansi.org/standards/nfpa/nfpa20012025);
  [NFPA TIA 2001-25-1](https://docinfofiles.nfpa.org/files/AboutTheCodes/2001/TIA_2001_25_1.pdf).
  Official page: https://www.nfpa.org/codes-and-standards/nfpa-2001-standard-development/2001
  (URL unverified).
- **NFPA 72, National Fire Alarm and Signaling Code, 2025 edition**
  (NFPA). PARTLY: 2025 edition named in an NFPA 2001 committee report as
  the referenced edition. Scope: detection, notification, ECS and
  supervising-station requirements; governs detector spacing, NAC and
  interface quantities. —
  [NFPA 2001 Second Draft report](https://docinfofiles.nfpa.org/files/AboutTheCodes/2001/2001_F2024_GFE_AAA_SD_SRReport.pdf).
  Official page: https://www.nfpa.org/codes-and-standards/nfpa-72-standard-development/72
  (URL unverified).
- **NFPA 76, Standard for the Fire Protection of Telecommunications
  Facilities, 2024 edition** (NFPA). PARTLY: retailers list 2024 as
  current; 2020 ed. superseded 15 Jan 2024; Li-ion battery requirements
  reportedly moved out to NFPA 855. No 2027 edition found. Scope: fire
  protection of telecom/network facilities incl. early-warning and
  very-early-warning detection. —
  [NSAI shop NFPA 76:2024](https://shop.standards.ie/en-ie/standards/nfpa-76-2024-829570_saig_nfpa_nfpa_3362064);
  [Fire-Police-EMS NFPA 76-2024](https://www.fire-police-ems.com/NFPA76-2024.shtml);
  [NFPA 76 First Draft report](https://docinfofiles.nfpa.org/files/AboutTheCodes/76/76_F2023_TEL_AAA_FRReport.pdf).
  Caution: one ANSI page labels 2024 most recent but describes 2020.
- **ISO 14520-1:2023, Gaseous fire-extinguishing systems — Physical
  properties and system design — Part 1: General requirements**
  (ISO/TC 21/SC 8). VERIFIED: 4th edition, Feb 2023, cancels 2015.
  Scope: design, installation, testing and maintenance of total-flooding
  systems using non-conducting, residue-free agents (not CO2, not
  explosion suppression); agent-specific parts follow. —
  [ISO 14520-1:2023](https://www.iso.org/standard/79444.html).
- **EN 54-20:2006 (+AC:2008), Fire detection and fire alarm systems —
  Part 20: Aspirating smoke detectors** (CEN). VERIFIED current: DIN
  lists DIN EN 54-20:2009-02 (EN 54-20:2006 + AC:2008); prEN 54-20:2014
  draft withdrawn; a "prEN 54-20 rev" review closed Aug 2021 with no
  published successor found. Scope: requirements, tests and sensitivity
  classes (A/B/C) for ASD/VESDA-type detectors. —
  [DIN Media DIN EN 54-20](https://dinmedia.de/en/standard/din-en-54-20/115703955);
  [SIS SS-EN 54-20:2006](https://www.sis.se/en/produkter/environment-health-protection-safety/protection-against-fire/fire-protection/ssen54202006/);
  [BSI BS EN 54-20](https://knowledge.bsigroup.com/products/bs-en-54-20-fire-detection-and-fire-alarm-systems-part-20-aspirating-smoke-detectors);
  [Genorma prEN 54-20 rev](https://genorma.com/en/standards/pren-54-20-rev).

#### Electrical (Volume 3)

- **NFPA 70, National Electrical Code, 2026 edition** (NFPA). VERIFIED
  (ANSI webstore) as most recent; 2023 ed. superseded. Article 645
  (Information Technology Equipment) retained: per a building-official
  forum post (secondary, verify), alternative wiring allowances now sit
  in 645.5 and the 645.10 EPO requirement applies only if those
  allowances are used; informational notes updated to latest NFPA 75.
  NEC reorganization continues toward 2029. Article 646 (Modular Data
  Centers) not examined this session. —
  [ANSI Webstore NFPA 70-2026](https://webstore.ansi.org/standards/nfpa/nfpa702026);
  [Building Code Forum on 2026 Art. 645](https://www.thebuildingcodeforum.com/forum/threads/understanding-the-2026-nec-changes-to-article-645.38834/latest);
  [IAEI: proposed 2026 NEC changes](https://iaeimagazine.org/columns/nfpa-code-talk/look-at-proposed-2026-nec-changes/).
- **IEC 61439-2:2020, Low-voltage switchgear and controlgear assemblies
  — Part 2: Power switchgear and controlgear assemblies** (IEC TC 121).
  UNVERIFIED edition (believed Ed. 3.0, 2020; companion IEC
  61439-1:2020 confirmed on CSA store). Scope: design verification and
  routine verification of PSC assemblies (main LV switchboards). —
  [CSA store IEC 61439-1:2020](https://www.csagroup.org/store/product/iec_032338/);
  [CERN standards list](https://section-mpc.web.cern.ch/node/339).
- **IEC 60364-1:2025, Low-voltage electrical installations — Part 1:
  Fundamental principles, assessment of general characteristics,
  definitions** (IEC TC 64). VERIFIED: Ed. 6.0, 5 Sep 2025, replaces
  Ed. 5 (2005); fully restructured and renumbered. —
  [IEC Webstore 60364-1](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/63699).
- **IEC 60364-5-52, Selection and erection of electrical equipment —
  Wiring systems** — UNVERIFIED (believed Ed. 3.0, 2009, with later
  corrigenda/IEC 60364-5-52:2009+AMD1 status not checked). Scope:
  wiring-system selection, current-carrying capacity and installation
  method tables used for cable sizing and containment takeoff.
- **IEC 62271-200:2021, High-voltage switchgear and controlgear — Part
  200: AC metal-enclosed switchgear and controlgear for rated voltages
  above 1 kV and up to and including 52 kV** (IEC SC 17C). VERIFIED:
  Ed. 3.0, 27 May 2021; AMD1:2024 published (consolidated Ed. 3.1). —
  [IEC Webstore 62271-200](https://webstore.iec.ch/publication/63466);
  [VDE Ed. 3.1 info](https://vde-verlag.de/iec-normen/preview-pdf/info_iec62271-200{ed3.1}en.pdf).
- **IEC 61850 series, Communication networks and systems for power
  utility automation** — UNVERIFIED this session (multi-part series;
  cite as series, e.g. IEC 61850 SER, with edition of the specific part
  used, e.g. 61850-7-4). Scope: substation automation data models and
  GOOSE/MMS messaging; relevant to MV substation protection/EPMS scope.
  Catalogue: https://webstore.iec.ch (search "61850"; URL unverified).
- **IEC 62040-3:2021, Uninterruptible power systems (UPS) — Part 3:
  Method of specifying the performance and test requirements** (IEC
  SC 22H). VERIFIED: Ed. 3.0, 21 Apr 2021; efficiency annex now
  normative. Scope: UPS performance classification (VFI/VI/VFD) and
  type/routine/factory tests (FAT basis). —
  [IEC Webstore 62040-3](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/60140).
- **IEC 62305-1:2024 to -4:2024, Protection against lightning** (IEC
  TC 81). VERIFIED: Part 1 Ed. 3.0, 12 Sep 2024, replaces 2010; Part 3
  2024 confirmed; series pack IEC 62305-SER:2024 lists Parts 1–4 at
  2024. BSI advises adopting the full Ed. 3 set together (risk
  assessment under Part 2). Scope: general principles, risk management,
  physical damage/LPS, and electrical/electronic systems (SPD) within
  structures. —
  [IEC Webstore 62305-1](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/27136);
  [IEC Webstore 62305-3](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/33680);
  [ANSI 62305 SER 2024](https://webstore.ansi.org/standards/iec/iec62305ed2024);
  [BSI Part 1 Ed. 3](https://knowledge.bsigroup.com/products/protection-against-lightning-general-principles-3).
- **EN 50272-2 → IEC 62485-2:2010 / EN IEC 62485-2:2018, Safety
  requirements for secondary batteries and battery installations — Part
  2: Stationary batteries.** VERIFIED: IEC Ed. 1.0 (16 Jun 2010) still
  current; European adoption EN IEC 62485-2:2018 (identical) replaces
  EN 50272-2:2001. Scope: stationary batteries up to DC 1 500 V;
  ventilation (hydrogen), electrolyte and electrical hazards — drives
  battery-room ventilation and spill containment items. —
  [IEC Webstore 62485-2](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/7091);
  [BSI BS EN IEC 62485-2](https://knowledge.bsigroup.com/products/safety-requirements-for-secondary-batteries-and-battery-installations-stationary-batteries-1);
  [NSAI EN IEC 62485-2:2018](https://shop.standards.ie/en-ie/standards/en-iec-62485-2-2018-353464_saig_cenelec_cenelec_806241).
  Conflict: batterydesign.net cites a "2016" edition — not supported by
  IEC record; treat as error.
- **ANSI/CAN/UL 9540:2023, Energy Storage Systems and Equipment, 3rd
  edition** (UL Standards & Engagement). VERIFIED by ULSE press release
  (28 Jun 2023); other sources say April 2023 and effective Sep 2024
  for new certifications (conflict on date). Scope: product safety
  listing of ESS (incl. Li-ion UPS cabinets); referenced by NFPA 855. —
  [ULSE third edition release](https://ulse.org/news/ulse-publishes-third-edition-ul-9540-energy-storage-systems-and-equipment);
  [SCC record](https://scc-ccn.ca/standardsdb/standards/4031957);
  [ANSI UL 9540-2023](https://webstore.ansi.org/standards/ul/ansiul95402023).
- **UL 9540A, Test Method for Evaluating Thermal Runaway Fire
  Propagation in Battery Energy Storage Systems** (ULSE). VERIFIED 5th
  ed. March 2025 (UL e-book); 6th edition reported published 13 Mar
  2026 (Intertek blog; UL says it tests to both 5th and 6th) — PARTLY,
  confirm on ULSE catalogue. Scope: cell/module/unit/installation-level
  thermal-runaway tests that set separation, suppression and explosion
  control requirements under NFPA 855/IFC. —
  [UL 9540A page](https://www.ul.com/services/ul-9540a-test-method);
  [Intertek 2026 update](https://www.intertek.com/blog/2026/03-23-understanding-the-2026-update-to-ul-9540a/);
  [Solar Power World Apr 2025](https://www.solarpowerworldonline.com/2025/04/ul-enhances-9540a-battery-testing-to-better-address-ess-industry-of-the-future/).
