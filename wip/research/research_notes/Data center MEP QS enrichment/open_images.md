# Openly Licensed Images of Data Center MEP Equipment (for a CC BY-SA 4.0 book)

Method: every licence, author, resolution and direct URL below was read from the Wikimedia Commons file-page metadata (MediaWiki `imageinfo`/`extmetadata` API, which renders the licence template on the file page itself) on 2026-11-23. No files were downloaded. Photos were NOT viewed, so "people/logos" notes come from file descriptions, filenames and categories; a visual check is still needed before each image goes into the book. NC, ND, GFDL-only, "Attribution"-custom and "Copyrighted free use" files were left out.

## Candidate register (42 primary candidates + 20 alternates)

### Takeaway
Wikimedia Commons can supply verified PD, CC0, CC BY and CC BY-SA images for nearly every requested subject. Strongest coverage: generators, UPS/VRLA batteries, raised floors, cable trays, clean agent cylinders and earthing bars. Weakest: busway inside a live data hall, Li-ion UPS racks, CDUs/cold plates and hot-aisle containment. For those, only near substitutes were found (generic busway, a utility BESS, an immersion tank, liquid-cooled HPC cabinets, a cold-aisle photo).

### Cited Findings
Attribution strings use the TASL pattern (Title, Author, Source, Licence). Where the file page carries an author-requested attribution, that wording is used.

## Agency and other open sources: licence terms checked

### Takeaway
DVIDS images are generally US-government public domain, with endorsement, trademark and privacy conditions. The NREL (now NLR) Image Gallery uses a custom, revocable licence that is NOT compatible with CC BY-SA, so NREL gallery photos should be excluded. Open Compute Project media have no single clear CC licence. ORNL's Flickr photos (CC BY 2.0) were the most useful national-lab source found.

### Cited Findings
- DVIDS: works of US government employees are generally not eligible for US copyright. Users must not imply DoD endorsement, should show the non-endorsement disclaimer, and should note that third-party copyrights, trademarks/insignia and personal publicity rights may still apply — [DVIDS copyright](https://www.dvidshub.net/about/copyright)
- NREL/NLR Image Gallery: photos by NREL staff are copyright NREL/Alliance for Sustainable Energy LLC. Gallery use falls under a custom agreement that requires credit plus the image number — [NREL editorial standards (via search)](https://www.nrel.gov/comm-standards/editorial/photo-image-and-video-credits)
- The NLR gallery agreement forbids selling or trading the content and products whose main value is the content. It requires prior consent to feed content into AI/ML tools, can be terminated by Alliance at any time, is non-assignable, and does not mention Creative Commons. This makes it incompatible with redistribution under CC BY-SA 4.0 — [NLR Image Gallery User Agreement](https://www.nlr.gov/news/image-gallery-user-agreement)
- Commons files of the ESIF / Peregrine supercomputer from the DOE Flickr stream are tagged "PD US DOE". One ESIF file credits "Dennis Schroeder, National Renewable Energy Laboratory", an NREL contractor photographer, so these PD tags are unreliable — [Commons Category:Energy Systems Integration Facility](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Energy_Systems_Integration_Facility); [Peregrine file](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:U.S._Department_of_Energy_-_Science_-_298_029_005_(31681202785).jpg)
- Open Compute Project: search results show conflicting licence statements (a wiki page under CC BY 4.0, an event video policy citing CC BY-SA 3.0). OCP logos need written permission — [OCP video policy](https://www.opencompute.org/wiki/Events/VideoPolicy); [OCP brand guidelines](https://www.opencompute.org/about/brand-guidelines/)
- US federal PD items that did verify: the Capitol Power Plant chillers photo (US Architect of the Capitol) — [file](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Chilled_Water_Efficiency_(28471938834).jpg)
- Commons category "Data center power rooms" holds about 50 CC BY-SA 3.0 photos by one photographer (Robert.Harker): ATS, EPO, UPS rows, battery bus bar, utility transformer and main breakers. It is a single consistent set for Volume 3 — [Category:Data center power rooms](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Data_center_power_rooms)
- Commons categories "Wikimedia servers in Ashburn" (RobH, CC BY-SA 3.0) and "Wikimedia servers in Carrollton" (Wikimedia Foundation / CyrusOne, CC BY-SA 3.0) give dozens of data-hall interiors — [Ashburn](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Wikimedia_servers_in_Ashburn); [Carrollton](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Wikimedia_servers_in_Carrollton)

### Inferences
- With NREL excluded, the best liquid-cooling sources are ORNL Flickr (Frontier, Summit; CC BY 2.0) and vendor-uploaded CC BY-SA images (Asperitas). Vendor uploads carry product branding.
- DVIDS photos can be used, but each needs the DoD non-endorsement line and a check for insignia. The book's credits page should hold a generic disclaimer for these.

### Gaps
- The DVIDS, NASA, LBNL and NIST image libraries were not searched item by item in this pass. Only Commons copies of federal images were checked. A DVIDS search for "generator", "data center" and "switchgear" (military facility power) could add PD options.
- The OCP site-wide media licence is unresolved. Ask OCP directly before using OCP photos.
- Flickr CC search (outside Commons) was not done.

## Coverage gaps by subject

### Takeaway
Seven of the requested subjects have only near substitutes or none: busway in a data hall, Li-ion UPS, CDUs, cold plates, hot-aisle containment, a dedicated LV switchboard close-up, and generator day tanks or bulk fuel tanks. ### Inferences
- Many good candidates show visible brands (Siemens, Schneider, MGE, ASCO, Fenwal, INERGEN, VESDA, HPE Cray, Tesvolt, Kohler, Perkins, LU-VE). CC licences do not clear trademarks. Incidental appearance in an editorial book is normally acceptable, but captions should not suggest endorsement.

### Gaps

- Frames were not viewed, so whether people or logos are visible is unconfirmed for every item.
- Commons "Electrical switchgear" and "Uninterruptible power supplies" category names returned no members; the working names are "Medium-voltage switchgear" and "Uninterruptible power supply".
