The register includes only public domain (PD), CC0, CC BY and CC BY-SA
files. NC, ND, GFDL-only, custom "Attribution" and "Copyrighted free

are excluded because their licence is incompatible with CC BY-SA.

All items have status **candidate**.

## Licence and usage notes

- **PD and CC0**: credit is a courtesy and not legally required.
- **CC BY**: requires attribution, a licence link, and a note of any
  changes made.
- **CC BY-SA 2.0/3.0**: adaptations may be relicensed under a later
  BY-SA version. This depends on the legal code's later-version clause,
  which was not re-checked this pass, so confirm it at
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/legalcode.
- **Trademarks**: CC licences do not clear trademarks. Captions must
  not imply endorsement.

### Doubtful-licence flags carried over

  contractor photo. Do not use until a federal photographer is
  confirmed.
- **A21**: a vendor graphic. Licence read as CC BY-SA 4.0, but the
